Support

Help with Melofy, the music-quiz game.

For bugs, account issues, or general questions, email us at trustsoftwareapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Profile → Settings → Delete account, or see our Account & Data Deletion page.
  • A song clip won't play. Check your connection and try again — preview clips are streamed live and can occasionally fail to load. If it keeps happening, email us with the playlist and track name.
  • I found a bug or have feedback. Email us a description of what happened, and a screenshot if you have one — it helps a lot.
