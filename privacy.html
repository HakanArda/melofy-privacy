2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the game (create games, score answers, run challenges).
  • To maintain your profile, XP, achievements, and leaderboard standing.
  • To show region-relevant content.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve Melofy.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Service providers & third parties

  • Supabase — our backend: authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Deezer — supplies music preview clips and artist search results. Playback of previews is subject to Deezer's terms.
  • Google & Apple — only if you choose social sign-in, to authenticate you.

4. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (see below), your account and associated data are removed. Limited analytics events may be retained for up to 7 days after deletion to preserve the integrity of usage metrics, after which they are permanently purged.

5. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and its data at any time, directly in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. See our Account & Data Deletion page for details.

6. Children

Melofy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Songs marked explicit by our music provider are hidden from quiz questions by default for every player, and can only be re-enabled by a player who confirms they are 18 or older (see "Date of birth" above).

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email trustsoftwareapp@gmail.com.
